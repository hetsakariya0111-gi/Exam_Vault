### 💡 Section 3: 3 Marks (Conceptual Breakdown)

#### Q1. Explain the components of an ER Model with brief descriptions.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Entity: A real-world object like a Student or Product identified independently.
* Attribute: The specific properties that describe an entity, such as Student Age.
* Relationship: The logical association showing how two or more entities connect together.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### Q2. Differentiate between a Strong Entity and a Weak Entity with examples.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* A strong entity contains a primary key and exists independently, like a Loan entity.
* A weak entity lacks a complete key and depends on a strong entity, like a Payment entity.
* Weak entities use a double rectangle symbol and require a partial key identifier.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
